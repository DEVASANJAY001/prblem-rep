# Hackathon & Open Innovation Mastery

*Stuckhere Academy, Subject 7: win challenges, ship working prototypes and turn events into real pilots*

## Chapter 0: Course Overview

### 0.1 What This Subject Covers

#### 0.1.1 The one-line idea

Hackathons and open-innovation challenges are the fastest route from a verified problem to a real audience, a mentor, a pilot or a prize. This subject teaches how to prepare, build, present and follow up so the event leads somewhere.

#### 0.1.2 What you will be able to do

- Pick the right hackathon or challenge for your skills and goals
- Read a problem statement the way judges do
- Form a balanced team and plan a 24 to 48 hour sprint
- Build a focused, demo-ready prototype
- Write strong documentation and submissions
- Pitch confidently and handle judges' questions
- Convert event contacts into pilots, internships or a startup

### 0.2 Who This Is For

#### 0.2.1 Students and first-time participants

#### 0.2.2 Experienced participants who want to win more often

#### 0.2.3 Mentors, organisers and challenge sponsors

### 0.3 Prerequisites
