# Solution Design & MVP Building

*Stuckhere Academy, Subject 4: turn a validated, sized problem into a tested product people will actually use*

## Chapter 0: Course Overview

### 0.1 What This Subject Covers

#### 0.1.1 The one-line idea

A verified problem still needs a well-designed answer. This subject teaches a human-centered way to go from problem to prototype to pilot without wasting months on features nobody needs.

#### 0.1.2 What you will be able to do

- Build user personas and journey maps from real interviews
- Reframe a problem into clear design questions
- Generate, compare and prioritise solution ideas
- Wireframe and prototype quickly
- Define and ship a minimum viable product
- Test with users and run a pilot with a real customer
- Pitch the result to judges, sponsors and customers

### 0.2 The Five-Stage Solver Roadmap

#### 0.2.1 Stage 1: Understand

#### 0.2.2 Stage 2: Define

#### 0.2.3 Stage 3: Ideate and prioritise

#### 0.2.4 Stage 4: Prototype and test

#### 0.2.5 Stage 5: Pilot and iterate

### 0.3 Prerequisites

#### 0.3.1 Subject 1: Problem Discovery & Validation

#### 0.3.2 Subject 2: Market Sizing & Opportunity Analysis

#### 0.3.3 Subject 3 (Full-Stack Cloud) is helpful but not required; no-code paths are covered
