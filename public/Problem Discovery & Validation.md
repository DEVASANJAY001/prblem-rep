# Problem Discovery & Validation

*Stuckhere Academy, Subject 1: from raw friction to a verified, buildable problem*

## Chapter 0: Course Overview

### 0.1 What This Subject Covers

#### 0.1.1 The one-line idea

Most builders start with a solution and hunt for a problem. This subject teaches the reverse: find a real, painful, recurring problem first, prove it, then build.

#### 0.1.2 What you will be able to do

- Spot problems in real workplaces and daily life
- Interview operators without pitching
- Document a problem as a structured dossier
- Score pain objectively (0 to 100)
- Decide whether a problem deserves a solution

### 0.2 Who This Is For

#### 0.2.1 Students and hackathon teams

#### 0.2.2 Early founders and indie builders

#### 0.2.3 Practitioners who want to report their own pain

### 0.3 How to Use This Material

#### 0.3.1 Suggested path (8 to 10 hours total)

#### 0.3.2 Chapter format: Concept, Example, Exercise, Checkpoint Quiz

#### 0.3.3 Using it with the Stuckhere Problem Hub and AI Solver Copilot
