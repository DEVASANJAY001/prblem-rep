# Startup Fundamentals

*Stuckhere Academy, Subject 6: from validated prototype to a real, sustainable venture*

## Chapter 0: Course Overview

### 0.1 What This Subject Covers

#### 0.1.1 The one-line idea

A good solution to a verified problem is not yet a company. This subject covers the business, legal, financial and people foundations you need to turn a pilot into a sustainable venture.

#### 0.1.2 What you will be able to do

- Choose a business model and pricing strategy
- Set up a team, roles and equity fairly
- Understand legal structures, IP and basic compliance
- Plan finances and runway
- Explore funding options and prepare for investors
- Build a go-to-market plan and early sales pipeline
- Track the metrics that matter and decide when to scale, pivot or stop

### 0.2 Important Note

#### 0.2.1 This course is educational, not legal, tax or financial advice

#### 0.2.2 Rules change by country and over time; confirm current requirements with qualified professionals and official government sources

### 0.3 Prerequisites

#### 0.3.1 Subjects 1 to 4 (problem, market, solution) recommended

#### 0.3.2 No finance or legal background needed

### 0.4 How to Use This Material

#### 0.4.1 Suggested path (12 to 16 hours with practice)

#### 0.4.2 Chapter format: Concept, Example, Template, Exercise, Checkpoint Quiz

#### 0.4.3 Building one venture plan across all chapters
