# Market Sizing & Opportunity Analysis

*Stuckhere Academy, Subject 2: how big is the problem, and is it worth solving?*

## Chapter 0: Course Overview

### 0.1 What This Subject Covers

#### 0.1.1 The one-line idea

A verified problem is only a good opportunity if enough people will pay enough, often enough. Market sizing turns a Pain Score into a number you can defend.

#### 0.1.2 What you will be able to do

- Estimate TAM, SAM and SOM bottom-up from real inputs
- Choose a defensible price and usage frequency
- Test your numbers with ranges and sensitivity analysis
- Present the opportunity in a one-page summary
